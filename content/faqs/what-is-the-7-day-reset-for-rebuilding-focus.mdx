question: What is the 7-day reset for rebuilding focus?
answer: >-
  The 7-day reset is an observation exercise. Each day, note one thing you moved
  forward, one thing that helped you regulate or recover, and one thing that
  connected you with a person, activity, or value that matters to you. At the
  end of the week, review what became easier and what you may want to continue,
  change, or stop.
