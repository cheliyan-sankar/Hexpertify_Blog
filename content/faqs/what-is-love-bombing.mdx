question: What is love bombing?
answer: >-
  Love bombing is when someone overwhelms you with excessive attention,
  affection, praise, gifts, constant communication, or intense promises about
  the future, particularly very early in a relationship. It can become
  concerning when the intensity is later followed by control, criticism, or
  manipulation.
