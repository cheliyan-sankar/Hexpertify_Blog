question: How can I tell if I am making progress?
answer: >-
  Progress does not always mean feeling completely better. Look for changes in
  everyday functioning, such as returning to a routine, concentrating for
  slightly longer, avoiding less, completing small commitments, recovering
  faster after difficult moments, asking for support earlier, or reconnecting
  with people and activities.
